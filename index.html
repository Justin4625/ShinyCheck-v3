          Shiny hunting 101

          Rare colors. Real patience.

          A shiny Pokémon is a rare, differently colored version of a Pokémon. It sparkles when it appears, and that's all it does: it's no stronger, just rare. Shiny hunters go after one on purpose, meeting the same Pokémon again and again, hatching eggs or clearing outbreaks until one sparkles.

          • Charizardblack instead of orange
          • Gyaradosred instead of blue
          • Umbreonblue rings instead of yellow
          • Rayquazablack instead of green
          1 in 4,096

          The base odds

          That's the chance for any Pokémon you meet to be shiny, in every game since X & Y. Before that it was 1 in 8,192.

          1 in 512

          Methods stack up

          The Shiny Charm, the Masuda Method (breeding two Pokémon from games in different languages), outbreaks, SOS chains, the DexNav, the Friend Safari and Sparkling Power all add rolls. In Scarlet & Violet the best wild setup gets you to 1 in 512.

          63%

          Luck, not a countdown

          Odds don't count down. After exactly as many encounters as the odds, about 63% of hunters have found theirs, and the rest keep going.

          What's inside

          Built by a shiny hunter, for long hunts.

          Shiny Dex

          Your whole shiny collection

          All 1,081 Pokémon and forms in one living dex. A card lights up as soon as you've logged a shiny of it in any game, so you can see at a glance what's still missing.

          Hunt Deck

          A counter made for the thousandth encounter

          • +1 with a big thumb button or the space bar
          • A timer that keeps running when you close it
          • Pick your method and bonuses, and the odds are worked out for you
          • A luck meter, phases and a pop-out mini window
          Games

          Every game's own dex

          Each game shows its regional dexes the way they are in the game, with Pokémon from outside the dex in their own tab. Shiny-locked Pokémon are marked and don't count against you.
